    Privacy Policy

    Traceable I.T. Ltd  |  Last updated: 5 November 2026

    Traceable I.T. Ltd ("Traceable I.T.", "we", "us", "our") is an I.T. consultancy and managed services firm based in Calabar, Nigeria. We take the privacy of our website visitors, prospects and clients seriously. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and the rights you have under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 ("NDPA") and other applicable laws.

    1. Who We Are

    For the personal data described in this policy, Traceable I.T. Ltd is the data controller. You can contact us at info@traceableit.com or on +234 916 997 2339.

    2. Information We Collect

    • Information you give us: your name, company, job title, email address, phone number, service interests and the content of any message you send through our contact form, email or WhatsApp.
    • Client engagement information: contracts, billing details, authorised contacts and the technical details we need to deliver our services (for example network diagrams, system configurations, asset inventories and access records).
    • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and referring website, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    • To respond to enquiries, prepare proposals and provide consultations.
    • To deliver, support, monitor and document the I.T. services you engage us for.
    • To manage accounts, invoicing, payments and contractual obligations.
    • To send service updates, monthly I.T. health reports and, where permitted, information about our services. You can opt out of marketing at any time.
    • To maintain the security of our systems, prevent fraud and misuse, and improve our website and services.
    • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

    4. Lawful Basis for Processing

    We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis under the NDPA: your consent; the performance of a contract with you or steps taken at your request before entering a contract; compliance with a legal obligation; or our legitimate interests (such as securing our systems and improving our services), provided these do not override your rights and freedoms.

    5. Client Systems and Data Processing

    When we install, manage or support a client's networks, servers, cloud environments or devices, we may incidentally access data held on those systems. In that situation the client remains the data controller and we act as a data processor. We access only what is necessary to perform the agreed services, act on the client's documented instructions, keep that data confidential, and handle it in line with our service agreement and a data processing agreement where required.

    6. Sharing Your Information

    We do not sell your personal data. We may share it only with:

    • Service providers who help us operate our business (for example cloud hosting, email, communication, accounting and security tools), bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
    • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and insurers.
    • Regulators, courts or law enforcement where we are legally required to do so.
    • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or restructuring, subject to this policy.

    7. International Transfers

    Some of our providers (such as Microsoft, Google or Amazon) may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred abroad, we take steps required by the NDPA to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection, such as contractual safeguards or transfers to jurisdictions with adequate protection.

    8. Data Security

    Security is our business. We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encryption where appropriate, multi-factor authentication, network segmentation, monitoring, regular backups and staff confidentiality obligations. No system is completely secure, but if a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected individuals within the timeframes required by law.

    9. Data Retention

    We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including to meet legal, accounting and contractual requirements. Enquiry data is generally kept for up to 24 months after our last contact; client and billing records are kept for the period required by Nigerian law and our service agreements. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it securely.

    10. Cookies

    Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to make the site work, remember your preferences and understand how visitors use it. You can accept cookies through our cookie notice and can block or delete them at any time in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

    11. Your Rights

    Under the NDPA, you have the right to:

    • Be informed about how your data is used and to access the data we hold about you.
    • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
    • Request deletion of your data where there is no legitimate reason for us to keep it.
    • Restrict or object to processing, including direct marketing.
    • Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format (data portability).
    • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.
    • Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you.

    To exercise any of these rights, email info@traceableit.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and will respond within the period required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

    12. Children's Privacy

    Our services are intended for businesses and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

    13. Third-Party Links

    Our website may link to third-party sites such as WhatsApp or social media platforms. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to read their policies.

    14. Changes to This Policy

    We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on our website with the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated to clients directly where appropriate.
